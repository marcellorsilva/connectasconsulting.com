The challenge

Your business shouldn't run on disconnected systems.

Five platforms, a stack of spreadsheets and a group chat hold most growing companies together. Every handoff means retyping, chasing and guessing.

Disconnected departments

Sales, operations and finance each work from their own version of the truth.

Duplicate data entry

The same customer and job typed into three systems, by three people.

Manual reporting

Every report starts with exporting spreadsheets and stitching them together.

Limited visibility

Managers find out about problems after they become expensive.

The solution

One connected ecosystem. Built around your business.

We design and develop a custom system that connects your people, departments, customers and operations, across web and mobile, so your business runs with clarity.

Conceptual illustration of an integrated architecture. The actual modules are defined for each client.

The Connected Business Experience

Experience a connected workflow.

Choose an area of the company and watch one action update every department that depends on it.

Sales receives a new order

The order placed on the website enters the system, registers the customer and starts every next step.

  1. WebsiteOrder submitted
  2. SalesDeal confirmed
  3. OperationsWork order created
  4. FinanceInvoice prepared
  5. Customer appStatus available
Illustrative flow. In real systems each update follows your business rules, permissions and configured integrations.

The field team completes a job

The crew closes the job in the app. Photos and sign-off reach the office, and everyone downstream moves on.

  1. Field appJob completed with photos
  2. OperationsJob closed and checked
  3. FinanceFinal invoice sent
  4. Customer appCompletion and review request
  5. ManagementWeekly report updated
Illustrative flow. In real systems each update follows your business rules, permissions and configured integrations.

Finance receives a payment

The customer pays online. The invoice, sales commission, next project phase and cash report update together.

  1. Client portalPayment made online
  2. FinanceInvoice marked as paid
  3. SalesCommission updated
  4. OperationsNext phase released
  5. ManagementCash report updated
Illustrative flow. In real systems each update follows your business rules, permissions and configured integrations.
