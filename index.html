How we work

From first conversation to a connected operation.

  1. 1DiscoverA free discovery call about your operation, goals and where work gets stuck.
  2. 2Define & PrototypeProcess map, priorities and initial scope, then screens designed under your brand for your approval.
  3. 3Build & IntegrateBuilt in stages and connected to your website and the tools you keep.
  4. 4Validate & LaunchYou review each stage; data is moved and teams are trained before go-live.
  5. 5Support & ImproveA monthly plan for hosting, monitoring, support and new features.
Can we start with a smaller process?

Yes. Projects are built in stages, so the first stage can focus on the process that costs your team the most time.

How do our existing systems fit in?

We map the tools you use today and connect them where they allow it. What to keep, connect or replace is decided with you during scoping.

How does our team validate what we build?

You approve the screens before development and review each stage as it is delivered.

What happens after launch?

A monthly plan covers hosting, monitoring, support and new features as your business changes.
