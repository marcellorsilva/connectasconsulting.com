Questions

Answered plainly.

Something else on your mind? Write to us at info@connectasconsulting.com.

Which countries do you work in?

The United States, Brazil and Guyana — in both directions. Most projects connect two of them, for example a Brazilian company opening in Guyana or a U.S. company entering Brazil.

Can I hire just one service?

Yes. Many clients start with a business plan or a company registration and add other services later. Each project has its own proposal and scope.

Do you provide legal or tax advice?

We coordinate that work with independent licensed lawyers and accountants in each country, and keep the whole process moving. Connectas does not replace them.

How long does it take to open a company?

It depends on the country, the type of company and the licences involved. Your plan includes a realistic timeline before you commit.

Do you work in Portuguese and English?

Yes. Our team works in both languages, and documents, websites and campaigns can be delivered in either or both.

How are fees charged?

Consulting, relocation, websites and marketing are priced per project in a written proposal. Our software platform is a monthly subscription.
