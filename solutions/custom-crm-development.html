Questions

Frequently asked.

Can you connect the CRM to our website forms?

Yes. Every form, booking and quote request becomes a lead with its source attached.

We already use a CRM. Do we need to replace it?

Not always. Sometimes the right answer is to integrate it. The discovery call is where we work that out.

Will salespeople use it on their phones?

Yes. The CRM can come with an iOS and Android app or a mobile-friendly web version.
