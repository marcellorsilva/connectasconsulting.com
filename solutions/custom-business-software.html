Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this a product we subscribe to?

No. We design and build a platform for your company. Hosting, support and improvements are offered afterwards as a monthly plan.

Can we start with one department?

Yes. Many companies start with sales or operations and add departments over time, on the same platform.

What happens to our current data?

We move customers, projects and history from your spreadsheets and current tools as part of the launch.
