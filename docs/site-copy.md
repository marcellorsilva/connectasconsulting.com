## TECHNOLOGY (new page — menu item "Technology")

**Eyebrow:** Connectas Technology
**Title:** Software that runs relocation and property-management companies.
**Intro:** Beyond the websites and apps we build for each client, Connectas offers its own platform: a complete system for relocation and property-management companies, delivered under each customer's brand and sold as a monthly subscription.

### The platform
- **Relocation management** — Cases, assignees and families, documents, timelines and messages, with portals for the client's HR team and for every assignee.
- **Property management** — Leases, inspections with photo reports, work orders and portals for landlords, agents and service partners.
- **Housekeeping & stays** — Occupancy calendar, bookings for rotational staff, cleaning tasks and a mobile app for the housekeeping team.
- **Finance & client funds** — Invoices, bills, advance funds and approvals, with amounts visible only to those who should see them.
- **Reports & allowances** — Expiry reports for leases and documents, cost-of-living allowance (COLA) calculations and client dashboards.
- **White-label systems & apps** — Web portal plus iPhone and Android apps under the customer's own brand, with sign-in by Face ID.

### White-label systems & apps
Your logo, your colours, your domain, your system and your app in the stores. Your clients see your brand — Connectas stays behind the scenes.

### Pricing
A monthly subscription, by module. You pay only for the modules you switch on. Prices are in US dollars and are always shown before you pay.
- **Base subscription (required)** — Monthly fee plus an amount per active assignee. HR and assignee portals, cases, documents, messages, users, roles and audit log, your brand and domain.
- **Add-on modules (optional)** — Each with its own monthly price: Property management, Housekeeping & stays, Furniture, Finance, Client funds, COLA. SMS alerts and assistant requests billed by use.
- **Your own app (one-time + monthly)** — Setup fee plus a monthly fee to publish and maintain your branded apps in the App Store and Google Play.

**How payment works.** Subscriptions are charged automatically every month to a credit or debit card. Card payments are processed by Stripe — card details never pass through our systems. Modules can be added from inside the platform and start right away. A free trial may be offered before the first payment. Any module, or the whole plan, can be cancelled from inside the platform with 30 days' notice — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy. A quote for your company is available on request.

### Security
- **Separate data** — Each customer's data is isolated from every other customer's, enforced by the database itself.
- **Confirmed access** — Access by invitation only. Nobody gets in before confirming their e-mail. 2-step verification for staff and HR, Face ID on the apps.
- **Right people, right data** — Access by role: HR, assignees, landlords and partners only see what belongs to them. Every change is recorded.
- **Payments by Stripe** — Card details are entered only on Stripe's secure pages. We never see or store card numbers.
