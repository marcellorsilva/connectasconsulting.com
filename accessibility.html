Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Connectas Enterprises LLC wants connectasconsulting.com to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we have done

  • Clear structure with headings, landmarks and a “skip to content” link.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Strong colour contrast in black and white.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus.
  • Animations that stop for people who set “reduce motion” on their device.
  • Forms with labels and clear error messages.
  • The site is available in English and Portuguese.

Known limitations

Some logos from clients and partners are decorative and have no text description. Articles may link to third-party sites that we do not control.

Feedback

If you find a barrier on this site, or need information in another format, write to info@connectasconsulting.com. We reply within two business days and will do our best to help.
