Project demonstrationKitchen, Bath & Home ImprovementIntegrated project

Kitchen & Bath Company

A connected platform for a kitchen and bath company, unifying showroom, sales, design, orders and installation.

The challenge

Inspiration, showroom visits, designs, supplier orders and installation dates lived in separate tools, notebooks and messages.

The solution

A website that captures inspiration and leads, connected to a sales inbox, project tracking from design to installation and a hub for the team.

What this enables

One view of every client, from the first saved idea to the final installation.

How it flows

  1. 1Inspiration saved on the website
  2. 2Lead in the sales inbox
  3. 3Design and estimate
  4. 4Approval and deposit
  5. 5Installation scheduled
  6. 6Client follows in the portal

Key capabilities

Inspiration gallery and visualizerSales inbox with lead contextDesign and project trackingTrade and partner portalTeam hub for operations, finance and ownersTeam chat and meetings

Project demonstrationHospitality & ChartersIntegrated project

Yacht Charter Company

One platform for charter bookings, owners, crews, maintenance and finance, on the web and in iOS and Android apps.

The challenge

Bookings, owner approvals, crew assignments, vessel documents and payments ran through calls, spreadsheets and messages.

The solution

A unified platform with role-based apps for the office, owners, captains and clients, all working from the same charter.

What this enables

Everyone works from the same booking, from reservation to return to the dock.

How it flows

  1. 1Booking request
  2. 2Owner approval
  3. 3Captain and crew assigned
  4. 4Client completes guest details
  5. 5Departure readiness checked
  6. 6Owner statement

Key capabilities

Reservations and fleet calendarOwner approvals and statementsCaptain and crew dayClient charter appMaintenance and defectsVessel documents and compliance

Project demonstrationCorporate Relocation Operations

Relocation & Property Company

A system for corporate relocation and property management, connecting HR teams, relocating employees and families, landlords and inspections.

The challenge

Each relocation involves HR, employees and families, landlords, agents and inspectors, often coordinated over email and messaging apps.

The solution

A central portal for cases, properties, leases, inspections and documents, with the right view for each party.

What this enables

One place to see where every relocation and every property stands.

How it flows

  1. 1Case opened with HR
  2. 2Home and lease tracked
  3. 3Move-in inspection
  4. 4Resident reports a repair
  5. 5Work order and visit
  6. 6HR reporting

Key capabilities

Relocation case managementProperty and lease trackingInspections with photosMaintenance requestsDocument and compliance trackingReporting for HR teams
