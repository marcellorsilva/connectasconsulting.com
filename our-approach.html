The process

Six steps, one team.

Free · 20 to 30 minutes

Discovery call

We learn how your company works today, where it's getting complicated and what success looks like.

Separate scope and price

Process mapping and architecture

We sit with each department, map every handoff, tool and channel, and design the architecture.

Approved by you

Design under your brand

Screens, website and apps that look like your company, reviewed before we build.

In stages

Build and integrate

Monthly plan

Support and grow

Pricing

No fixed prices. A clear proposal.

A truly custom platform can't be priced from a menu. Your investment comes from a diagnostic of your processes, users, integrations, security, apps and support requirements.

The first conversation is free. The process mapping is a separate step with its own scope and price, and it produces a written proposal you can decide on with confidence.

What we promise

Honest commitments.

We won't promise information that is 100% correct in every circumstance, because quality also depends on source data and outside integrations. Here is what we do promise.

Consistency

Validation rules and one trusted source for every piece of data.

Traceability

Role-based permissions and an audit trail for every change.

Fewer errors

Information entered once and shared automatically, instead of retyped.
