Our approach

From discovery to deployment.

Your company already makes money the way it works today. We start by listening to every department, then automate the friction so your people can focus on what sells.

Book a Discovery Call Explore our solutions
The process

Five steps, one team.

Free · 20 to 30 minutes

Discover

A conversation about how your company works today, where work gets stuck and what success looks like.

Separate scope and price

Define & Prototype

We map your workflows with the people responsible for each department, then deliver a process map, priorities and an initial scope, with screens designed under your brand for your approval.

In stages

Build & Integrate

We build the platform in stages and connect your website and the tools you keep.

Reviewed by you

Validate & Launch

You review each stage as it is delivered. We move your data, train each team and stay close through the first weeks.

Monthly plan

Support & Improve

Hosting, monitoring, support and new features as your business changes.

Pricing

No fixed prices. A clear proposal.

A truly custom platform can't be priced from a menu. Your investment comes from a diagnostic of your processes, users, integrations, security, apps and support requirements.

The first conversation is free. The process mapping is a separate step with its own scope and price, and it produces a written proposal you can decide on with confidence.

What we promise

Honest commitments.

We won't promise information that is 100% correct in every circumstance, because quality also depends on source data and outside integrations. Here is what we do promise.

Consistency

Validation rules and one trusted source for every piece of data.

Traceability

Role-based permissions and an audit trail for every change.

Fewer errors

Information entered once and shared automatically, instead of retyped.
