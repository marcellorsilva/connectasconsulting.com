Pricing

Projects by proposal. The platform by subscription.

Websites, systems and apps are priced per project in a written proposal. The platform is a monthly subscription by module, in US dollars, always shown before you pay.

Required

Base subscription

Monthly fee plus an amount per active user or record, depending on the solution.

  • Client and team portals, documents, messages
  • Users, roles and audit log
  • Your brand and domain
Optional

Add-on modules

Each module has its own monthly price.

  • CRM · Operations · Bookings
  • Finance & billing · Partner portals · Reports
  • SMS alerts and assistant requests, billed by use
One-time + monthly

Your own app

Setup fee plus a monthly fee to publish and maintain your branded apps in the App Store and Google Play.

How payment works. Subscriptions are charged automatically every month to a credit or debit card. Card payments are processed by Stripe — card details never pass through our systems. Modules can be added from inside the platform and start right away. A free trial may be offered before the first payment. If a payment fails, the customer has 7 days to pay before access is paused; data is always kept. Any module, or the whole plan, can be cancelled from inside the platform with 30 days' notice — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy. A quote for your company is available on request.
