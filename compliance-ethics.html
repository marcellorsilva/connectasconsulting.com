Compliance & Ethics Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Connectas Enterprises LLC builds software that companies trust with their customers, their data and their numbers, so integrity is not optional. This policy applies to everyone who works for or on behalf of Connectas.

1. Zero tolerance for bribery and corruption

We never offer, promise, give, request or accept bribes, kickbacks or improper payments, directly or through others — including “facilitation payments” to speed up permits or approvals. We comply with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and with any other law that applies.

2. Government interactions

All dealings with public officials are transparent, documented and made through official channels. Government fees are paid only against official receipts.

3. Gifts and hospitality

Modest gifts and hospitality are acceptable only when they are reasonable, infrequent, transparent and never linked to a decision. Nothing is given to public officials without prior approval.

4. Anti-money laundering and sanctions

We know our clients and partners, check where funds come from on significant transactions, and do not work with sanctioned people, companies or countries. Suspicious activity is reported as the law requires.

5. Conflicts of interest

Anyone with a personal, family or financial interest that could affect a decision must disclose it before acting. Where Connectas and companies related to its owners provide services to the same client, this is disclosed and pricing is fair.

6. Fair competition and honest marketing

We compete on quality. We do not make false claims, and the figures we publish are based on our records or on identified public sources.

7. Confidentiality and data protection

Client information is confidential and protected under our Data Protection Policy. We never share one client’s information with another.

8. Partners and suppliers

We expect lawyers, accountants, agents and suppliers working with us to follow the same standards, and we end relationships that do not.

9. Speaking up

Concerns can be raised in good faith, confidentially, by writing to info@connectasconsulting.com. Nobody will suffer retaliation for reporting a concern in good faith. Every report is reviewed and, where needed, acted upon.

10. Consequences

Breaches of this policy may lead to the end of a working relationship or contract and, where the law requires, reporting to the authorities.

Based in Coral Springs, Florida
