Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Connectas Enterprises LLC uses cookies on connectasconsulting.com.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your choices and, with your permission, measure how it is used.

2. The cookies we use

CategoryWhat it doesDefault
EssentialKeep the site secure and working, remember your language and your cookie choice (cnx_consent, kept for 12 months). Our hosting provider may set technical cookies needed to deliver the pages.Always on
MeasurementAnonymous statistics about visits, so we can improve the site.Off until you allow
MarketingMeasure campaigns and show relevant content on other platforms.Off until you allow

Today the site only uses essential cookies. Measurement and marketing tools will only run if you switch them on.

3. Your choices

When you first visit, you can accept all cookies, keep only the essential ones, or choose by category. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie preferences button at the bottom of every page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site from working.

4. Third parties

If we add measurement or marketing tools, their providers may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. We will list them here.

5. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: info@connectasconsulting.com.
